About SatQuery AI

SatQuery AI is a natural-language-first vision-language assistant for earth observation and satellite imagery (SIH 2026 Problem Statement SIH26167).

Ask natural questions like "Describe this scene", "Where is the water?", or "Has construction increased?". Behind the scenes, domain-adapted specialist models (GeoRSCLIP, OpenCLIP VQA, Bi-temporal change detection, Optical-SAR fusion) process the imagery and generate visual evidence.

Team AstraNex • ISO/OGC geospatial standard compliance, auditable execution traces, and explainable AI confidence scoring.
